Product art direction — exploration
Four surfaces, not one card
Twenty-eight screens currently share a single container. These four show the alternative — the same design language producing genuinely different compositions per product moment. Main prototype and Flutter untouched.
Ground
Lists, records, long text. No container at all — hairline dividers only.
Inset group
Settings, label/value pairs. One rounded group, internal dividers, no per-row card.
Raised card
Reserved for the one focal object on a screen. Scarcity is what makes it read.
Sheet
Map, filters, confirmations. Content behind stays visible.
Two corrections carried into the language
Ring geometry is one spec, product-wide: 216 / 152 / 107 at 1.42 ratio, scaled per moment — onboarding 1.00 / 1.24 / 1.62 / 0.90, Matched 0.90. Scale and ratio travel; the centre does not. The group centres on whatever the screen's focal point actually is — (252, 300) in onboarding because the type block is left-aligned and the offset creates tension; (196, 176) on Matched because the composition is centred on the two avatars. Every use states its scale and its centre, and where the ring appears something real must sit on one of its radii (on Matched, both avatars at r 68.5). Screens with no ring moment get no ring; it is never card decoration.
One muted text token, not a ramp: #6E6A62 — measured 5.04:1 on the #FBF7F1 ground and 5.38:1 on white — carries every muted string under 18px: micro-labels, timestamps, secondary copy and inactive nav labels. Micro-labels separate from body copy by weight and letter-spacing, never by lightness. #A0968A / #B0A996 / #9C9C98 / #D3C9BC are non-text only — dividers, rules, inactive dots. Document chrome on the darker #E8E4DC ground uses #5A564F (5.75:1). On red fields the same rule applies against the gradient's lightest stop (#7E1C26), not its average: #E0A8AF (4.99:1) for micro-labels, #E9BFC4 (5.42:1) for body, #D9AFB4 (5.16:1) for tertiary. Control borders on dark sit at rgba(251,230,232,.55) to clear 3:1. Superseded: #B0A996 at 2.20:1, #A0968A at 2.73:1, #9C9C98 at 2.76:1 and #7D7469 at 4.30:1 all failed AA at these sizes.
